# Hero video

The current clip is `ho-chi-minh-archive.mp4`: a 24-second silent loop edited
from verified public-domain historical photographs of Hồ Chí Minh and events in
1945. It is a moving presentation of still images, not original historical film
footage. It autoplays muted, loops, and remains behind the exhibition while the
visitor scrolls.

The loop uses: Ba Đình Square (2 September 1945), Hồ Chí Minh with the OSS
Deer Team (1945), Hồ Chí Minh and Võ Nguyên Giáp (2 September 1945), and a
portrait of Hồ Chí Minh (circa 1947). Their source and licence records are in
`public/images/ASSETS.md`. Any later replacement must be directly relevant to
Hồ Chí Minh, the formation of his thought, or the historical events presented by
the exhibition. Keep its source/licence record with the course assets.
